Hello, World!

This is the website of the Casual Coding Club of the HPI. We meet about every week on Thursday in the room H-E.7/8. Most people arrive at 16:00.

We noticed that during our studies, personal projects often fall flat because of time. The Casual Coding Club meetings are an opportunity to work on side projects. Your are welcome to bring your own projects or be inspired by those of other people! There are snacks, drinks, and of course other people that can give feedback and help with problems.

If you are interested, just drop by our meetings or send us a mail at klub-casual-coding-sprecher@hpi.de.

Hi! We are the Casual Coding Club of the HPI. We meet about every week on Thursday in the room H-E.7/8. Most people arrive at 16:00.

We noticed that during our studies, personal projects often fall flat because of time. Our meetings are an opportunity to work on side projects. You are welcome to bring your own projects or be inspired by those of other people! There are snacks, drinks, and of course other people that can give feedback and help with problems.

If you are interested, just drop by at one of our meetings or send a mail to klub-casual-coding-sprecher@hpi.de.
